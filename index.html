QUALITY, AFFORDABLE, AND OPEN EDUCATION

The University of Mindanao (UM), the largest private, non-sectarian university in Mindanao, is dedicated to providing quality, affordable, and open education. UM welcomes all learners without entrance examinations or barriers to admission, staying true to its mission of democratizing access to education. It provides a dynamic learning environment and upholds the highest standards in instruction, research, extension, and production. Learn More about UM →

PQA LEVEL IV

The University of Mindanao (UM) is the first academic institution in the Philippines to be awarded the Philippine Quality Award (PQA) Level IV for Performance Excellence. This prestigious award recognizes UM's outstanding organizational performance and commitment to quality.

The PQA Level IV award signifies that UM has fully met the standards set in the PQA requirements, demonstrating a culture of performance excellence.

VISION ▼
The University of Mindanao envisions to be a leading globally engaged university creating sustainable impact in society.
MISSION ▼
The University of Mindanao seeks to provide a dynamic learning environment through the highest standard of instruction, research, extension, and production in a private non-sectarian institution committed to democratizing access to education.
CORE VALUES ▼

Excellence: We are committed to world-class customer service and quality as we excel for the mutual success of our stakeholders.

Honesty and Integrity: Our organization establishes utmost trust and ensures transparency in dealing with our stakeholders.

Innovation: We always think outside the box to be of prime service to our stakeholders.

Teamwork: We believe that the whole is greater than the sum of its parts.
