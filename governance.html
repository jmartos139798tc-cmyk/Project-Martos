University Governance

The University of Mindanao operates under a comprehensive governance structure designed to ensure academic excellence, institutional integrity, and responsible stewardship of resources. Our governance model emphasizes transparency, accountability, and participatory decision-making.

As an autonomous institution recognized by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), the University exercises flexibility in academic and administrative matters while maintaining the highest standards of educational quality.

Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees serves as the highest governing body of the University, responsible for setting strategic direction, ensuring fiscal responsibility, and upholding the institution's mission and values.

View Board of Trustees

University Administration

The University administration is led by the President, who serves as the chief executive officer responsible for day-to-day operations and implementation of Board policies.

Office of the President

The President provides strategic leadership and oversees all aspects of university operations, including academic affairs, student services, administrative functions, and external relations.

Policies

The University maintains comprehensive policies and guidelines to ensure consistent, fair, and transparent operations across all aspects of institutional life.
